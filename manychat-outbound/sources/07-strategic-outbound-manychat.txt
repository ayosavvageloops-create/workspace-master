      Strategic Outbound (ManyChat)
      Scripts and automation checklist for running outbound at scale on top of your proven DM framework.



    What Strategic Outbound Is
•    A mix of cold outreach + inbound DMs, run at scale using ManyChat automations.
•    Sits on top of your existing DM qualifying framework — not a replacement for it.
•    Most valuable once you already have a proven offer, ICP, and a DM process that converts manually.

    BUILD THE MANUAL VERSION FIRST
    Automation should mirror what already works for you manually. Don't automate a process you haven't already proven with
    real conversations.


    1      Strategic Outbound Scripts
    Same core principles as Loom outreach and DM qualifying — format shifts to fit an automated flow. Every message: ONE job,
    move the lead closer to the call.


        Personalized                  Open                                                 Budget                  Book
                                                                Qualify
          Opener                     Question                                              Signal                  Call



    Stage 1: Personalized Opener
      “Yo [Name], what's good? Saw [specific reference — comment, post, track].”
    Stage 2: Open Question
      “You working on anything right now, or just looking for beats/sounds?”
    Stage 3: Qualify
      “What's the goal with the next few songs? What's your process right now that's making it hard?”
    Stage 4: Budget Signal (Indirect)
      “Have you ever invested in a producer or service like this before? How'd that go?”
    Never ask directly how much money they have — this reads spending history without an interrogation.
    Stage 5: Book the Call
      “Let's hop on a call to keep this going — no selling, just want to understand where you're at.”

    2      Checklist of ManyChat Automations to Use

•    Keyword-trigger auto-reply — comment/DM your bio keyword triggers the opener + open question.
•    Qualifying question sequence — automate the situation/goals/pain-point questions as a guided flow.
•    Budget signal step — the indirect “have you invested before” question, every time, automatically.
•    Call booking hand-off — auto-deliver your booking link once a lead clears qualifying.
•    Follow-up / no-response nurture — automated follow-up for leads who opened but went quiet.

    WHERE THIS FITS
    Full outbound-at-scale is more relevant once you already have consistent leads and need to manage volume. For your first
    $1,000–$2,000: get the manual DM flow working perfectly first, then layer automation on top.
