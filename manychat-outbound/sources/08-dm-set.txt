      DM SET — STAGE 8 · QUALIFY THE LEAD


      The DM Setting Framework
      The exact qualifying flow for your DMs, and which funnel to run depending on where the lead came from.



    The Job of the DM
•    The DM is the first low-friction step into a real conversation.
•    Goal of a DM = a REPLY, not a sale. Whole point: get them on a call.
•    Every message has one job: move them one step closer to the call.

     TWO MISTAKES THAT KILL DM CONVERSIONS
     1) Going silent, waiting for “I'm ready to pay you.” 2) Pitching way too early and scaring the lead off. The DM's purpose is to
     confirm it's worth both your time and build trust — nothing more.


    1      DM Setting Script & Framework


                                       Open                                                   Budget                      Book
            Opener                                                Qualify
                                      Question                                                Signal                      Call



    Step 1: The Opener
    Human and specific — never copy-pasted. Reference exactly what they commented, liked, or watched.
      “Yo, saw you commented on that beat vid — you working on anything right now, or just looking for sounds?”
    No backstory (cold keyword DM)? Open simple, let the CTA's purpose guide the tone:
      “Yo, what's good bro?”
    Step 2: Open Question
      “You working on a project right now, or just looking for beats?”
    Step 3: Qualifying
      “What's the goal with the next few songs? — What's your process right now that's making that hard?”
    Step 4: Budget Signaling (Indirect, Never Direct)
     WHY THIS MATTERS
     $50 in the bank saying “I don't have the money” isn't an objection — it's true. But someone who told you they make
     $2,000/month, then says “I don't have the money” on the call — THAT you can objection handle, because you already know
     they do.

      “Have you ever invested in something like this before? How'd that go?”
      “Would you be open to something like that if it actually got you there faster?”
    Step 5: Book the Call
    Real gap between pain points/situation and goals = your cue. Not before, not later.
      “Let's hop on a call to keep this going — talk more about where you're at and how I can help. No selling on this call.”

     Even for warm leads, running the two-call system when starting out gets far more ICP data without needing to qualify as hard

    Which Funnel Do I Use? Quick Reference
    Signal                         Lead Type A (Cold)                            Lead Type B (Warm)

    Where they came from           Your Loom outreach video                      Commented/DM'd your bio keyword

    Trust level                    Lowest — first contact                        ~10x higher — already engaged with you

    Calls needed                   2 (Discovery, then Sales)                     Often 1 (Sales only)

    First call's job               Pure discovery, zero pitch                    Qualify fast, move to pitch

    Quick Recap — Do Not Move On Until Done
•    DM opener, open question, qualifying, and budget-signal scripts all written.
•    Know which funnel (2-call vs 1-call) to run based on lead source.
•    Comfortable asking about money indirectly, never directly.
•    Clear on the cue to book the call: a real, visible gap between pain points and goals.
